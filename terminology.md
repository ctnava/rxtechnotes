# 🧠 Medical Terminology (Enhanced & Corrected)

## 🧩 Structure of Medical Terms

| Component | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Root** | Foundation of the word; provides the core meaning. A term may contain multiple roots. |
| **Prefix** | Modifies the meaning; appears at the **beginning** of a term. |
| **Suffix** | Modifies the meaning; appears at the **end** of a term. |
| **Combining Vowel (CV)** | Connects word parts to improve pronunciation; most commonly **“o”**. |

### 🧪 Examples

- **hypo** (low, prefix) + **glyc** (sugar, root) + **emia** (blood, suffix) → **hypoglycemia**  
- **cardi** (heart, root) + **o** (CV) + **my** (muscle, root) + **o** (CV) + **pathy** (disease, suffix) → **cardiomyopathy**

## 🔗 Combining Vowel (CV)

- Used to make terms easier to pronounce.
- Most common CV is **“o”**.
- Used **between roots** or **between a root and suffix** (when the suffix begins with a consonant).

---

## 🔧 Prefixes & Suffixes

Prefixes and suffixes **modify** the root to give additional meaning.

- A term may have **one** or **none** of each.
- **Suffixes** are more common than prefixes.
