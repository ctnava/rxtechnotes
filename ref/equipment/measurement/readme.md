# Measuring Devices

Pharmacy technicians measure **solids by mass** and **liquids by volume**.  
Each measurement requires the correct device, proper technique, and awareness of the limitations of the equipment.

---

## Measuring Solid Masses ⚖️

Balances determine **mass** by measuring **weight** (the force exerted by gravity).  
Accurate weighing requires proper **calibration**, **environmental control**, and **handling technique**.

- Use balances for **powders, granules, excipients, and APIs**  
- Always use **weighing papers or boats** to protect the pan  
- Follow balance maintenance and calibration procedures to ensure accuracy

🔗 See: **[Weighing Equipment](./weighing/readme.md)**

---

## Measuring Liquids by Volume 🧪

Liquids are measured using **volumetric equipment**, which is calibrated either:

- **To Deliver (TD)** → delivers the exact volume when emptied  
- **To Contain (TC)** → contains the exact volume when filled to the mark  

Choosing the correct device depends on:

- Required **accuracy**  
- **Volume** being measured  
- **Viscosity** of the liquid  
- Whether the measurement is **critical** (e.g., potent drugs, pediatric doses)

🔗 See: **[Volumetric Equipment](./volumetric/readme.md)**

---

## Volume Contraction

`Mass is conserved; volume is not`

The final volume of a solution will not always be the sum of the individual volumes of liquid ingredients; admixtures of aqueous & nonaqueous solutions.
