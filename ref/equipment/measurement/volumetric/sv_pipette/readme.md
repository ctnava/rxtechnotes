# Pharmacy Equipment Manual: Volumetric Pipettes 🧪

Volumetric pipettes are **TD (To Deliver)** devices designed to deliver **one exact volume** with extremely high accuracy.  

## Step 1: Draw Liquid

Attach a **rubber bulb** (or pipette pump) to the top of the pipette.

- Squeeze the bulb to expel air  
- Gently release pressure to draw liquid **above** the calibration line  
- Do **not** draw liquid into the bulb  
- Keep the pipette **vertical** while filling

> 🛡️ **Safety:** Never mouth‑pipette. Always use a bulb or mechanical device.

![[Drawing Liquid](http://www.chymist.com/VOLUMETRIC%20Glassware%20LSM.pdf)](./draw.png)

## Step 2: Remove Pipette from Solution

- Remove the pipette from the container **while still applying slight suction**  
- Quickly transfer the pipette to a vertical position  
- Ensure no droplets cling to the outside of the pipette tip

## Step 3: Wipe the Tip

Use a **Kimwipe** or lint‑free tissue to gently wipe the **outside** of the tip.

- Removes excess droplets  
- Prevents volume errors  
- Do **not** touch the opening of the tip  
- Do **not** wick liquid out of the pipette

## Step 4: Drain Excess to Calibration Mark

Hold the pipette **perfectly vertical** over a waste beaker.

- Release pressure on the bulb  
- Allow liquid to drain **slowly** until the **bottom of the meniscus** aligns with the calibration mark  
- If a droplet clings to the outside of the tip, touch it to the **inside wall** of the waste beaker to remove it  
- Do **not** blow out liquid  
- Do **not** shake the pipette

> 📌 The calibration mark represents the **exact volume** the pipette is designed to deliver.

![[Draining Liquid](http://www.chymist.com/VOLUMETRIC%20Glassware%20LSM.pdf)](./drain.png)

## Step 5: Deliver the Liquid

Move the pipette to the receiving vessel.

- Touch the pipette tip to the **inner wall** of the receiving container  
- Allow the pipette to **drain by gravity**  
- Do **not** force out remaining liquid  
- Standard drain time: **30 seconds**  
- For viscous liquids: **up to 3 minutes**

> 🧠 **Important:** Volumetric pipettes are **TD, not blow‑out** devices.  
> Any liquid remaining in the tip after draining is **intended** and accounted for in calibration.
