# Topicals

Topical dosage forms are applied to the **skin or mucous membranes** for **local** or **systemic** effects.  

They may be **solutions, suspensions, emulsions, semisolids, or ointment bases** depending on therapeutic need.

---

## Lotions

Fluid, low‑viscosity **liquid topicals** used for:

- Lubrication  
- Cooling  
- Covering large or hairy areas  

Lotions may be:

- **Suspensions** (shake well)  
- **Emulsions** (o/w or w/o)

📌 *Lotions spread easily and dry quickly.*

---

## Creams

Creams are **emulsions** that exist as **very thick liquids or soft solids**.

Used for:

- **Emollient** effects (softening/smoothing skin)  
- Moisturizing  
- Delivering active drugs with good spreadability  

Characteristics:

- Easy to apply  
- Easy to remove  
- Less greasy than ointments  
- Can be **o/w** (most common) or **w/o**

📌 *Creams = emulsions with a soft, spreadable consistency.*

---

## Ointments

Ointments are **semisolid preparations**, often emulsions containing a **large proportion of base** with or without dispersed solids.

Used for:

- **Protectants**  
- **Emollients**  
- **Antiseptics**  
- **Antipruritics**  
- **Drug delivery** (active drug mixed into base)

Ointments vary widely depending on the **base type**

| Ointment Base Type | Water Content | Spreadability | Washability | Greasiness | Occlusive? | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Oleaginous (Hydrocarbon)** | Anhydrous | Difficult | Non‑washable | Greasy | Yes | White Petrolatum USP, White Ointment USP |
| **Absorption Bases (Anhydrous)** | Anhydrous | Difficult | Non‑washable | Greasy | Yes | Hydrophilic Petrolatum USP, Aquaphor |
| **Absorption Bases (W/O Emulsion)** | Hydrous | Moderate to Easy | Poorly washable | Greasy | Sometimes | Hydrous Lanolin, Eucerin, Nivea |
| **O/W Emulsion Bases (Cream Bases)** | Hydrous | Easy | Washable | Non‑greasy | No | Hydrophilic Ointment USP, Versabase |
| **Water‑Miscible Bases** | Hydrous | Moderate to Easy | Washable | Non‑greasy | No | PEG Ointment USP, Polybase |

- **More oil = more occlusive, more greasy, less washable; less drug penetration**  
- **More water = easier to spread, easier to wash off; more drug penetration**  
- **O/W = cream‑like**  
- **W/O = ointment‑like**

📌 *Occlusive bases trap moisture → good for dry, cracked skin.*
