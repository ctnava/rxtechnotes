# Pharmacy Settings

Pharmacy practice spans multiple environments, each with its own workflow, patient population, and operational focus. Understanding these settings helps technicians identify career paths and adapt their skills to different clinical and operational demands.

## Outpatient Settings

Outpatient care involves patients who receive medical services **without** being admitted to a hospital overnight. These settings focus on accessibility, patient counseling, and high‑volume prescription processing.

- [Community Pharmacies](./ref/settings/retail.md): Serve walk‑in patients; focus on dispensing, counseling, immunizations, and insurance processing.
- [Compounding Community Pharmacies](./ref/settings/compounding.md): Prepare customized medications (sterile or non‑sterile) tailored to individual patient needs.
- [Mail Order Pharmacies](./ref/settings/mailorder.md): High‑volume, centralized dispensing with automated systems; medications shipped directly to patients.
- [Home Health Care](./ref/settings/homehealth.md): Provide medications, supplies, and infusion therapies for patients receiving treatment at home.

---

## Inpatient Settings

Inpatient care involves patients who stay **overnight or longer** in a medical facility. These settings emphasize sterile preparation, medication distribution systems, and interdisciplinary collaboration.

- [Hospital Pharmacies](./ref/settings/hospital.md): Support acute‑care treatment; prepare sterile products, manage automated dispensing cabinets, and collaborate with clinical teams.
- [Long‑Term Care (LTC) Pharmacies](./ref/settings/ltc.md): Provide medications for residents in skilled nursing facilities, assisted living, and rehabilitation centers.

---

## Hybrid Settings

Hybrid environments blend inpatient and outpatient workflows, often requiring specialized training and strict regulatory compliance.

- [Nuclear Pharmacies](./ref/settings/nuclear.md): Prepare radiopharmaceuticals for diagnostic imaging and therapeutic procedures; operate under stringent safety and handling protocols.
