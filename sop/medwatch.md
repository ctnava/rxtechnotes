# **Reporting Adverse Drug Events (ADEs) to FDA MedWatch**

## **1. Purpose**

To provide a standardized procedure for identifying, documenting, and reporting **Adverse Drug Events (ADEs)**, product quality issues, and medication errors to the FDA’s **MedWatch** program using **Form FDA 3500**.

---

## **2. Scope**

This SOP applies to all pharmacy personnel involved in:

- Medication dispensing  
- Clinical monitoring  
- Quality assurance  
- Patient safety reporting  

Covers reporting for:

- Prescription & OTC drugs  
- Biologics  
- Medical devices  
- Dietary supplements  
- Cosmetics  
- Special nutritional products  

---

## **3. Definitions**

**Adverse Drug Event (ADE):**  
Any undesirable medical occurrence associated with the use of a medication, whether expected or unexpected.

**MedWatch:**  
The FDA’s Safety Information and Adverse Event Reporting Program.

**Form FDA 3500:**  
The voluntary reporting form for healthcare professionals to report ADEs, product problems, and medication errors.

**Serious ADE:**  
Includes death, life‑threatening events, hospitalization, disability, congenital anomaly, or events requiring intervention to prevent permanent harm.

---

## **4. Responsibilities**

### **Pharmacists**

- Evaluate the ADE for severity and causality  
- Determine if MedWatch reporting is required  
- Complete and submit Form FDA 3500  
- Document the event in the patient profile and internal reporting system  

### **Technicians**

- Gather preliminary information (patient details, product info, timeline)  
- Document the event in internal systems  
- Immediately notify the supervising pharmacist  

### **Pharmacy Manager / Medication Safety Officer**

- Ensure staff compliance with this SOP  
- Review ADE trends for quality improvement  
- Maintain records of all MedWatch submissions  

---

## **5. Procedure**

### **5.1 Identify and Document the ADE**

1. Collect the following information:
   - Patient demographics  
   - Drug name, strength, dosage form, NDC  
   - Lot number & expiration date (if available)  
   - Description of the event
     - if/ when treatment was sought
     - how the problem resolved (if applicable)
   - Onset time relative to drug administration  
   - Concomitant medications  
   - Relevant medical history  

2. Determine whether the event meets criteria for **serious ADE**.

3. Document the ADE in the pharmacy’s internal reporting system.

### **5.2 Determine Reporting Pathway**

Report to **FDA MedWatch** if the event involves:

- Serious ADE  
- Unexpected reaction  
- Product quality issue (e.g., contamination, incorrect strength)  
- Medication error with potential for harm  
- Device malfunction  

### **5.3 Submit Report to MedWatch**

#### **Option A — Online Submission (Preferred)**

Submit electronically via the FDA MedWatch portal:

🔗 **[MedWatch Online Voluntary Reporting (Form FDA 3500)](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/medwatch/)**  

Steps:

1. Select **“Report a Problem”**  
2. Choose **“Healthcare Professional”**  
   - Refer Patients to "Patient/Consumer" forms
3. Complete **Form FDA 3500** online  
4. Review for accuracy  
5. Submit electronically  
6. Save confirmation number for pharmacy records

#### **Option B — Download and Submit Form FDA 3500**

If online submission is unavailable:

🔗 **[Download Form FDA 3500 (PDF)](https://www.fda.gov/media/76299/download)**  

Submit completed form by:

- **Fax:** 1‑800‑FDA‑0178  
- **Mail:**  
  *MedWatch, 5600 Fishers Lane, Rockville, MD 20852‑9787*

### **5.4 Internal Documentation**

After submitting to MedWatch:

- Record the **MedWatch confirmation number**  
- Update the patient profile  
- Notify the Pharmacy Manager or Medication Safety Officer  
- File a copy of the report in the ADE log  

Retention requirement: **Minimum 3 years** (or per state law).

---

## **6. Training**

All pharmacy staff must receive annual training on:

- ADE identification  
- MedWatch reporting procedures  
- Use of Form FDA 3500  
- Documentation requirements
