## Legal Compliance Documents

### Safety Data Sheets (SDS)

`Formerly Materials Safety Data Sheets (MSDS)`

OSHA sets nationwide standards to keep pharmacy staff safe when working around chemicals, hazardous drugs, and potential exposure risks. Every pharmacy must maintain basic OSHA compliance, regardless of setting.

#### **Key Points for Technicians**

- Pharmacies must keep **Safety Data Sheets (SDS)** for all hazardous chemicals and drugs.
- Staff must receive **initial and periodic safety training** (e.g., chemical hazards, spill response).
- Employers must provide appropriate **PPE** at no cost (gloves, gowns, eye protection when required).
- Hazardous drugs (including chemotherapy agents) require **chemo‑rated PPE** and special handling.
- Pharmacies that administer vaccines must follow **Bloodborne Pathogens** requirements (e.g., sharps safety, exposure procedures).

#### **What SDS Are**

Safety Data Sheets explain:

- Hazards of a chemical or drug  
- Required PPE  
- Safe handling and storage  
- Spill and first‑aid instructions  

> ⚠️ All hazardous drugs and cleaning chemicals must have SDS accessible to staff.
